How Provident Fund works

Provident Fund (PF) is a retirement savings scheme: every month a percentage of your basic salary goes into the fund, and your employer usually adds the same percentage on top.

The fund earns interest, compounded monthly in this calculator. Your balance grows from three parts: what you pay, what your employer pays, and the interest on both.

This tool is a planning estimate. Real PF rules, interest rates and tax treatment vary by employer and scheme (government, private, or company-run fund). Confirm the exact rate and rules with your HR or fund office before making decisions.

How the numbers are worked out

Each month: your contribution + employer contribution are added to the balance, then one month of interest (annual rate ÷ 12) is added on the new balance. Your basic salary grows by the annual increment % at the start of each year after year 1. All amounts are rounded to the paisa (৳0.01) each month.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. Rates shown are the ones you enter — no official rate is assumed.
